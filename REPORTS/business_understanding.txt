# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional performance.

## 2. Business Problem

A retail business needs to understand which products and regions contribute most to its sales and profits. It also needs to investigate whether discounts are associated with lower profitability.

The purpose of this analysis is to turn sales data into useful information that can support better business decisions.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Calculate total sales and total profit.
2. Identify the best-performing products and categories.
3. Compare sales and profits across regions and cities.
4. Analyse the quantities of products sold.
5. Understand the distribution of payment methods.
6. Examine the relationship between discounts and profitability.
7. Provide data-supported recommendations to improve business performance.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel: Initial data inspection and analysis.
- Python: Programming and data analysis.
- Pandas: Data manipulation and analysis.
- SQL: Querying structured data where appropriate.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and visualisations.
- GitHub: Project version control and portfolio presentation.

## 5. Expected Deliverables

1. An inspected and validated dataset.
2. Python code for data analysis.
3. Excel analysis and summary tables.
4. An interactive Power BI dashboard.
5. A summary of key findings and recommendations.
6. Project documentation for GitHub.

## 6. Important Note

The findings and recommendations will be based on the actual dataset. The business questions and objectives may be refined after inspecting the data and validating the calculations.
